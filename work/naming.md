# Naming

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: candidates for the user to pick in `work/brand/logo-gallery.html`.
User instruction 2026-11-07: «названия на английском только».

## Inputs

- Mechanism (verb): count how far and how fast a settlement reaches markets, and recount when a link appears or disappears (road, flight, winter road, river, ferry).
- Domain terms: connectivity, accessibility, isochrone, graph, node, leg, portage, winter road, thaw, corridor.
- User: regional transport and economy analysts, the Ministry of Transport strategy team, the jury of case A2.
- Feeling after use: "I can see where the link breaks and what fixing it gives".
- Form: one latin word, lowercase, 3–8 letters, readable aloud by Russian speakers.

## Finalists (pending the user)

1. **Isochron.** The technical term for a line of equal travel time: precise for the jury, strong animated mark (growing rings).
2. **Portage.** The overland link between two rivers: exactly what the product looks for. Harder to pronounce for a Russian jury.
